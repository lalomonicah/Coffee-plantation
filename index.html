From Farm to Package

Each harvest moves through five carefully managed stages:

  1. Planting healthy coffee seedlings
  2. Cultivating and caring for the trees
  3. Harvesting ripe coffee cherries
  4. Processing and drying the beans
  5. Grading and packaging the finished coffee

Plantation Activities

Pruning
Removing selected branches encourages healthy growth, improves airflow and helps keep trees productive.
Harvesting
Ripe coffee cherries are picked at the right point in the season to preserve their quality and flavor.
Drying
After processing, beans are dried carefully to reduce moisture and prepare them for storage and grading.
